# Product Context

**Audience**: Recruiters, Engineering Peers, Hiring Managers.

**Register**: Brand (Design IS the product - this is a personal portfolio showcasing UI/UX sensitivity and frontend capability).

**Brand Personality**: Sophisticated, crisp, highly legible, intentional. It should feel like a premium digital editorial or a high-end agency site rather than a generic SaaS tool or a bloated AI mock.

**Anti-references**:
- Generic modern SaaS (no purple/blue gradient buttons, no scattered floating blobs).
- "AI Slop" (no Fraunces, Geist, or Space Grotesk overuse; no nested cards / Cardocalypse; no hero eyebrow chips).
- "Template" portfolio (no standard side-tab cards without breathing room).

**Aesthetic Direction**: Minimalist, structural, high-contrast. Rely on whitespace, strong typography hierarchy, and subtle structural dividers instead of boxed cards and heavy shadows.
